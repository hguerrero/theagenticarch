**The Agentic Architect** explains how agentic systems actually work—from APIs and MCP to event-driven architecture, governance, and the infrastructure behind useful agents.

**Architecture patterns for agentic systems people can trust.**

The focus is the practical engineering required to make agents discover capabilities, use the right context, act within policy, and behave reliably in production.

## What you’ll find here

- Agent design: orchestration, planning, evaluation, and tool use.
- APIs, events, MCP, and the machine-accessible foundations agents depend on.
- Identity, authorization, observability, governance, and other controls that make systems trustworthy.
- Field notes for architects, platform teams, product leaders, and builders moving from AI demos to durable products.
