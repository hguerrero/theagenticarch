The agentic era will not be won by the teams with the cleverest prompt. It will be won by organizations whose systems expose trustworthy, discoverable capabilities that agents can safely use.

That is the premise of **The Agentic Architect**: architecture patterns for agentic systems people can trust.

Models matter. But a capable model without the right context, tools, identity, and controls is still a limited agent. The hard—and interesting—work is building the architecture around intelligence so that it can understand an objective, act responsibly, and earn trust.

## Who this is for

This is for architects, API and platform teams, product leaders, and builders who are moving from AI demos to durable systems. It is a place to connect the technical foundations of the enterprise with the emerging ways agents can use them.

## What we’ll explore

- **Agent architecture:** orchestrators, planners, evaluators, and the choices that shape behavior.
- **Agent-accessible capabilities:** APIs, events, MCP tools, and context that agents can discover and compose.
- **Trust and operations:** identity, authorization, governance, observability, reliability, and cost control.
- **Field notes:** practical lessons from designing and operating agentic systems in the real world.
